# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, especially around real-world call
  handling practices that vary by market
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism on command syntax or wording
- Focusing on what's best for the agents who rely on this knowledge base

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Submitting deliberately false or misleading command syntax
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue tagged `conduct` or
contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly.
